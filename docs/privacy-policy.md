# JobHakken — Apply Copilot · Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

This policy covers the **JobHakken "Apply Copilot" browser extension**. Publish it at a stable URL
and enter that URL in the Chrome Web Store listing (Privacy tab). Keep it consistent with the
[store listing](./store-listing.md) and the product's privacy model.

## What the extension does

The extension autofills job-application forms, shows how well your résumé matches a posting, surfaces
visa-relevant signals, and tailors your résumé — working alongside the **JobHakken desktop app** on
your computer.

## What data the extension handles

- **Your profile / résumé / application details** (e.g. name, contact details, work history) — used
  **only** to autofill forms and produce job-fit and résumé-tailoring results.
- **The content of job postings you view**, to compute the match and fill the form.

The extension does **not** collect your browsing history, and does **not** track the pages you visit
beyond the job postings you actively use it on.

## Where your data goes

- **Your data stays on your device.** The extension exchanges it **only** with your **local JobHakken
  desktop app** over a loopback connection (`127.0.0.1`) on your own machine.
- We do **not** send your résumé, profile, or application content to JobHakken servers.
- We do **not** sell or rent your data, and we do **not** share it with third parties.
- We do **not** use your data for advertising, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to the
  extension's single purpose (helping you apply to jobs).

If the extension ever reports diagnostics, it is **metadata only** (e.g. feature used, counts) — never
your résumé, job, or personal content — and is disclosed separately.

## Permissions and why they're needed

- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — save your autofill profile and cache the bundled H-1B sponsor list
  locally in the browser.
- **Access to `127.0.0.1` / `localhost`** — communicate with your own JobHakken desktop app on your
  machine. No external servers are contacted.
- **Access to job-board / applicant-tracking sites** — detect postings and fill application forms on
  the pages where you apply.

## Data retention & control

Your data lives in the local desktop app and your browser's local storage. You can remove it by
clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling the extension and/or the desktop app.
