Firmware Engineer

Example Aerospace is hiring a Firmware Engineer to build embedded control systems for our next-generation avionics platform. You will own bring-up, driver development, and real-time scheduling across our sensor suite.

Eligibility: Must be a U.S. Citizen. This position requires an active security clearance (or the ability to obtain one). Applicants must be able to access export-controlled (ITAR) technical data.

  • 5+ years of embedded C/C++
  • Experience with RTOS and low-level debugging
  • Bachelor's in EE, CE, or related field
