Job description

cross-ING is an independent ISO 9001/13485-certified Swiss engineering company. The company offers a wide range of knowledge in the field of product development to its customers. cross-ING supports customers with many years of experience and globally connected, interdisciplinary expertise to create new things and generate added value. The cross-ING Group AG takes on administrative tasks in areas such as marketing, sales, and human resources.


We are looking for talented Embedded Software Engineers who would like to work in a dynamic company. At the moment, we may not have a suitable project for you, but things can change quickly, so sign up for our talent pool now. We will work on your profile so that we can definitely find you for our next project request.

Job requirements

Your Responsibilities

  • Develop embedded firmwares for our customers from various industries

  • Software development of industrial applications from requirement to rollout

  • Development of data security concepts

  • Architecture development with control systems, apps, cloud, databases, and embedded systems

  • Conceptual development of applications and co-development of software

  • Interface architecture and integration of embedded systems and GUI

  • Development and implementation of IoT solutions – with the opportunity to work in our Competence Center Digital if interested

  • On-site with customers or in internal projects, you are part of an interdisciplinary development team and take responsibility for assigned work packages

Your Qualifications

  • Practical experience in areas of digitalization (IoT, Software Development, Industry 4.0, etc.)

  • Excellent programming skills (C, C++, Python, Java, etc.)

  • Experience with blockchain, cyber security is advantageous

  • Experience in electronics development is desirable

  • Communicative and proactive personality

  • Structured way of working

  • Creative, customer-oriented, and independent team player with quick comprehension

  • Very good German and English language skills

  • Enjoy working in a team, thrive on interdisciplinary collaboration, and are highly committed to the success of our customers

What We Offer
Once a suitable initial project is available, we offer you a varied and responsible position in a permanent position with us. We ensure that you always receive challenging projects that match your profile and that you can learn a lot from your current skills. Our employment conditions provide contemporary framework conditions with attractive benefits.
