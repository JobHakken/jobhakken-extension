Advanced: matching operators
= equals · ^ starts-with · ! not · && and · || or · ( ) group

The condition is matched against the field’s label (case-insensitive). With no operator your phrase just has to be contained in the label — the operators are for the trickier cases:

  • notice periodlabel contains it — “What is your notice period?”
  • ^salarylabel starts with “salary” — matches “Salary expectations”, not “Desired base salary”
  • =gpalabel is exactly “GPA” — skips “What’s your GPA and major?”
  • salary && !currenthas “salary” but not “current” — fills “Expected salary”, skips “Current salary”
  • sponsor || visahas “sponsor” or “visa” — one rule covers both phrasings
  • (relocate || relocation) && !remotegrouped — a relocation question that isn’t about remote work

⚙Account & settings▾

👤 Your JobHakken account iSign in for managed AI and to sync across devices — the same login you use everywhere. Optional: the extension works without an account, and with your own AI key it's fully free.

💾 Back up & restore iSaves your profile, the answers you've taught the extension, every question it has seen, your résumés and cover-letter template to a single file on this computer. Reinstalling no longer costs you that. The file includes your AI key, so keep it private — don't attach it to an issue or paste it into a chat.

🤖 AI drafting (your own key) iAdd your own AI key to draft answers for open-ended questions — works without the desktop app, on any plan, at no cost to us. Get a key at openrouter.ai/keys (or use OpenAI). Kept in memory this session only, never sent to JobHakken.

🖥️ Desktop app — optional iLets you import your résumé and unlock AI features (résumé match, visa signal, tailored résumé). In the app, open Settings → Browser extension, turn it on, and copy the code here. Your data never leaves your computer. Don't have it? Download the app.
