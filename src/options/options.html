JobHakken — Profile & settings

Set up your profile once — it autofills job applications. The desktop app is optional (connect it to import your résumé + unlock AI).

👋 Getting started — three steps
  1. Add your info below (or Import it from the app).
  2. Open a job application in your browser.
  3. Click Autofill in the JobHakken toolbar popup.

Nothing is ever submitted for you — you review everything first. Your data stays on your computer.

🔗 Connect the JobHakken app — optional

Connecting the JobHakken desktop app lets you import your résumé and unlock AI features (résumé match, visa signal, tailored résumé). It’s optional — you can fill in your details by hand instead.

Don’t have the app yet? Download it here, open it, then come back to this tab.

🤖 AI drafting (your own key)

Add your own AI key to draft answers for open-ended application questions — works without the desktop app, on any plan, at no cost to us. Get a key at openrouter.ai/keys (or use OpenAI). Your key is kept in memory for this browser session only, never sent to JobHakken.

🖥 Desktop app (optional)

In the app, open Settings → Browser extension, turn it on, and copy the code here. Your data never leaves your computer.
